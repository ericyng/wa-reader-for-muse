Full Disk Access needed
WA Reader for Muse reads WhatsApp's message database on this Mac. macOS protects that file, so the app needs Full Disk Access. Nothing is exported until you grant it.
  1. Click Open System Settings — it takes you to Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.
  2. Find WA Reader for Muse in the list and switch it on. (If it's not listed, click + and pick it from Applications.)
  3. Come back here — this window notices on its own, usually within a few seconds.
Open System Settings Reveal app in Finder ⟳ Checking access…

Why a settings trip? Apple doesn't let apps ask for Full Disk Access with a pop-up — any app that needs it has to send you to Settings. This app only ever reads WhatsApp's database, and only the threads you choose. Because it is signed with a stable local certificate, this grant survives rebuilds and macOS upgrades.
